How the swarm works

  1. 1

    Parser & Researcher

    Normalises the input, redacts personal data, attaches industry benchmarks, scores data quality.

  2. 2

    Analyzer

    Computes monthly KPIs (ARPU, churn, CAC, conversion), period trends and anomalies.

  3. 3

    Refiner

    Corrects impossible values, scores confidence and ranks insights against benchmarks.

  4. 4

    Lead Generation & Marketing

    Maps the funnel, flags wasteful spend, suggests channels and target keywords.

  5. 5

    Consultant

    Scores review sentiment by feature area, finds market gaps and prioritises fixes.

  6. 6

    Visualizer

    Builds the interactive dashboard: KPI tiles, charts, insights and drill-down reviews.

Built-in guardrails

  • Strict input validation: field types, ranges, row and review limits.
  • Personal data (emails, phone numbers, card-like numbers) redacted before any analysis.
  • Review text containing instructions to the AI is flagged and kept out of AI prompts.
  • Each agent's output is checked for missing summaries and non-finite numbers.
  • Per-agent timeouts, one automatic retry, and a cap on concurrent analyses.
