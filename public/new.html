Upload files

Add spreadsheets or documents instead of typing. A CSV or Excel sheet with monthly figures (columns such as month, revenue, customers, marketing spend, leads, conversions, churned) fills the metrics. A sheet with a review, comment or feedback column adds reviews. Word (.docx), text and Markdown files are added as documents. PDF and the old .xls and .doc formats are not supported.

    Context
    Monthly metrics (CSV)

    Header row required. Needs month and revenue. Optional: customers, marketing_spend, leads, conversions, churned (churned = customers lost that month).

    Customer reviews

    One review per line. Prefix a star rating with 5| for a rating from 1 to 5. Optional.

    Live sources

    Public URLs to fetch when the analysis runs: RSS/Atom feeds, JSON review feeds, or review pages. One per line, up to 10. Private and local addresses are blocked, robots.txt is honoured, and each source is fetched once per run (use Live on the dashboard to refresh).

    Documents

    Paste reports, call notes or articles. Separate documents with a line of three dashes (---). Start a document with "# Title" to name it. Up to 20 documents, 50,000 characters each.
